# 🏥 STAT — Overview

## 🎯 Vision
**STAT** is a standalone, fully-3D, AAA-scalable game whose entire world is one **living hospital**.
"STAT" is the hospital word for *now* — and the game is about the weight of urgent decisions. It should
feel as premium and cinematic as a top-tier open-world title, but instead of a city it deeply simulates
a single hospital: patients arrive, staff work their shifts, resources deplete, and emergencies escalate
in real time. The player's core gameplay is **clinical reasoning and prioritization under pressure** —
investigate within a limited budget and clock, commit to a diagnosis, treat, and live with the
consequences — inside a hospital that reacts to every choice.

## 🕹️ Platforms & Fidelity
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux, PlayStation, Android, iOS — **one Unreal Engine 5 / C++
  codebase**, built per platform.
- **Scalable AAA:** original, best-possible-quality environment and character art on high-end hardware,
  scaled down through explicit tiers so the game still runs well on **mid-range PCs and mobile**. Top
  tier targets AAA fidelity; the mobile tier is a faithful, readable reduction — the same game, not the
  same pixels on a phone.

## 💎 Cool & Unique Features (the strong ideas)
1. **Diagnosis *is* the gameplay (the VEX clinical engine).** A budget-and-time-limited loop — read the
   case, examine in 3D, buy labs/imaging/consults, commit to a diagnosis, treat — scored on accuracy,
   appropriate investigation, resources retained, and avoided harm. *(First-principles: strip a hospital
   game to its real job — thinking like a clinician — and make that the loop, not a side mode.)*
2. **A living hospital simulation.** ER, ICU, OR, radiology, wards; NPC patients and staff with
   conditions, routines, and reactions; resources, beds, and reputation that actually move.
3. **Emergent crisis pressure.** Mass-casualty intake, outbreaks, power failures, a deteriorating
   patient mid-procedure — cases *collide* and force triage. *(Analogous domain: the "missions collide"
   tension of an open-world game, transplanted to an ER.)*
4. **Consequences that remember you.** Outcomes ripple through the hospital — trust, resources,
   unlocked departments, patient stories that continue. *(Data leverage: the case history is the moat.)*
5. **Deep options.** Roles (attending / resident / paramedic), campaign + endless sandbox, difficulty
   and assist sliders, optional co-op colleague, and best-in-class accessibility.
6. **Fully-3D, authentic, original art.** A believable hospital and a diverse cast of patients and
   staff, art-directed for atmosphere and readability across every platform tier.

## 🧭 Built To Our Skill Standards
Every part of STAT is designed and reviewed against the six installed skills in `.claude/commands/`,
used as the project's **governing protocols and acceptance criteria** (not just advice):
`/function` (behavior, wiring, performance), `/anatomy` (structure, flow, reachability),
`/style` (visual system, motion, accessibility), `/ideas` (feature quality bar),
`/dataman` (data & medical-case discipline), `/gamify` (motivation, scoring, anti-abuse).
Their laws, lenses, checklists, and zero-tolerance anti-patterns are the bar each stage must pass.

## ⚕️ Responsible Framing (non-negotiable)
STAT is **educational and entertainment only**. It is **not** clinical decision support, medical advice,
or a substitute for professional care. Clinical content is authored responsibly, labeled clearly, and
validated; the game never presents itself as a real diagnostic tool.
