# 🚓 STAT (Street Tactical Assault Team) - Overview

## 🎯 Vision
STAT is a standalone, cinematic open-world action game for Windows PC. It should feel expensive, dangerous, and reactive: dense city blocks, heavy vehicle handling, systemic police chases, mission pressure, and a world that responds to the player in real time.

## 💎 Cool & Unique Features
1. **Living City Simulation:** Traffic, civilians, police patrols, weather, and district heat all update dynamically.
2. **Mission Pressure:** Story jobs, side hustles, crew events, and pursuits can collide at once, forcing the player to choose priorities fast.
3. **Cinematic Combat & Driving:** Cover shooting, takedowns, traffic escapes, and destruction-heavy vehicle sequences feel weighty and premium.
4. **Crew Co-op:** A second player can join selected missions as backup driver, lookout, mechanic, or support shooter.
5. **VEX Protocol:** A standalone educational challenge mode where players solve clinical cases by purchasing tests from a limited budget and committing to a diagnosis.

## 📍 Placement
- **Platform:** Windows PC only.
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5 with C++.
- **Vibe:** Neon city, bass-heavy atmosphere, harsh lighting, chaotic action, and a polished AAA presentation.

## VEX Protocol Mode

`VEX Protocol` is available from the main menu and uses STAT's profile, scoring, difficulty, audio, accessibility, and save infrastructure. It is intentionally separated from the open-world campaign so its medical-education rules do not distort the fiction or pacing of the main game.

Each run presents a text-led clinical case, a finite virtual budget, purchasable labs, imaging, consults, and procedures, followed by a final diagnosis. The score rewards diagnostic accuracy, appropriate investigation, money retained, and avoidance of harmful or wasteful actions. This mode is educational only and must never be presented as clinical decision support.
